# Getting help

- **Chat and quick help:** join the [MALT Discord](https://discord.gg/DwSpsGjvFY). Ask in **#help**, talk development in **#dev-chat**, and come to office hours.
- **Questions you want answered for everyone:** ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/Division4Studios/multi-agent-local-team/discussions/categories/q-a). Answers there are searchable on the web; Discord is not.
- **Ideas:** talk them through in **#ideas** on [Discord](https://discord.gg/DwSpsGjvFY), then open a [feature request](https://github.com/Division4Studios/multi-agent-local-team/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) once the approach is agreed.
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Division4Studios/multi-agent-local-team/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with reproduction steps, your OS, your VS Code version and your MALT version.
- **A verdict you think is wrong:** open a [wrong review result](https://github.com/Division4Studios/multi-agent-local-team/issues/new?template=review_quality.yml) report.
- **Security issues**, including any way to make the reviewer ignore its rules: never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

MALT is maintained by volunteers. We read everything but can't promise response times.
